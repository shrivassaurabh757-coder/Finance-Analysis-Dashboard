# Finance-Analysis-Dashboard

Finance Analysis Dashboard | Power BI, SQL, Excel

Developed an interactive Finance Analysis Dashboard to analyze revenue, expenses, profit, and financial performance across different periods, departments, and business segments.

Key Responsibilities & Analysis:

Cleaned and transformed financial data using Excel and Power Query.
Used SQL to extract, filter, aggregate, and analyze financial transactions.
Created interactive Power BI dashboards to track revenue, expenses, profit, profit margin, and financial KPIs.
Analyzed monthly and yearly revenue and expense trends to identify business growth patterns.
Compared actual vs. budgeted performance to identify variances.
Performed category, department, and regional profitability analysis.
Created DAX measures for KPIs, YoY growth, profit margin, and variance analysis.
Added interactive filters, slicers, drill-downs, and charts for better business insights.
Identified high-performing and low-performing business segments to support data-driven decision-making.

Tools & Technologies:
Power BI | SQL | Excel | Power Query | DAX

Key KPIs:
Total Revenue | Total Expenses | Net Profit | Profit Margin | Budget Variance | YoY Growth | Revenue Growth
